Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Infonex Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it when you use our website at www.infonexsolutions.com or contact us for services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Your organisation name
  • Details about the services you enquire about
  • Any message content you send via our contact form or WhatsApp

We do not collect any information automatically beyond what your browser sends as standard (such as your IP address and browser type), and we do not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request
  • Send you quotes, proposals, or follow-up communications related to your enquiry
  • Maintain records of client engagements for service delivery purposes

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Confidentiality

All client engagements are handled with strict professional confidentiality. Where required, we enter into a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Rules of Engagement (RoE) before commencing any security work. Your data and any findings from assessments remain strictly private.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact and engagement information only for as long as necessary to deliver services and maintain accurate business records. If you wish to have your information removed from our records, please contact us and we will action this promptly.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent to any future communications at any time

6. Third-Party Services

Our contact form directs submissions to WhatsApp (operated by Meta). By using this form, your message content is processed through WhatsApp's platform, which has its own privacy policy. We use no other third-party data processors.

7. Security

As a cybersecurity firm, we take the security of your data seriously. All communications with us are handled through secure channels. We do not store form submissions on any external server.
